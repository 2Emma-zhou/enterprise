You can connect camera feeds to Tier0 and apply visual analytics to your live camera images.

## Connecting Cameras
1. In Tier0 Enterprise, go to **Analysis** > **Vision**.
2. Under the **Camera** tab, click **Add Camera**.

<img width={450} src="https://enterpriseimage.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/12.png" />

3. Enter the camera information and stream URL, test the connection, and then click **OK**.

## Editing Algorithms
1. Under the **Algorithm** tab, check the algorithm list and find one that fits your needs.
2. Click **Details** to view the algorithm details, click **Edit** when you need to update the model or add recognizable items.

<img width={450} src="https://enterpriseimage.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/13.png" />

3. Click **OK**.

## Analyzing Camera Feeds
1. Under the **Analysis Tasks** tab, click **Create Task**.
:::tip
You can also click **Use Algorithm** under the **Algorithm** tab to create a task based on the selected algorithm.
:::
2. Under **Recognition** tab, enter the task name, select the camera and algorithm.

<img width={450} src="https://enterpriseimage.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/14.png" />

3. Switch to the **Detection Area** tab, click the plus icon at the lower-right corner on the image, click on the image to draw a detection area.
4. Click **Finish** to finish the area drawing.

<img width={450} src="https://enterpriseimage.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/15.png" />

5. Under the **Schedule & output** tab, configure the analysis running schedule and output settings.
:::tip
Description of each parameter will be displayed when you hover over it.
:::

<img width={450} src="https://enterpriseimage.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/16.png" />

6. Click **Create** and the result can be viewed under the **Analysis Results** tab.
