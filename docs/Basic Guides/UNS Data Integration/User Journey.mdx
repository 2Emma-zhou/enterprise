Start with the two required steps shown in the diagram:

<img width={650} src="/img/user-journey-en.svg" alt="Build Data Models, then Connect Data; choose to Process, Sync, Use, or Analyze Data" />

- **Necessary**
    1. **Build Data Models** to organize your assets and define where data belongs.
    2. **Connect Data** to bring data from your sources into those models.

- **Optional**
    - **Process Data** to transform and prepare data with **Event Flow**.
    - **Sync Data** to share data with other Tier0 or make it available through OpenAPI and MQTT.
    - **Use Data** in applications and business scenarios.
    - **Analyze Data** to explore and visualize what the data reveals.
