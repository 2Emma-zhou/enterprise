The digital twin feature is available on the platform to help you visually build data models.

## Uploading Digital Twin Models
1. Log in to Tier0, select **Anchor** > **Model Library**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/62.png" />

2. Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/63.png" />, select a model file, and upload it to the platform.

## Mapping UNS Data to Digital Twin Models
:::info
There are 2 types of data mapping with 3D models.
- **Static Mapping**: Displays UNS data as a static tag on the scene. Often used in Gaussian Models (no movable parts but in need of data display) or static attributes of structured models (e.g., temperature of AC).
- **Motion Mapping**: Data mapped to the movable parts of the model, and the parts move according to the data change.
:::
1. After uploading the model, click the model to start mapping it to UNS data.
2. Select model nodes to be mapped, and click **OK**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/67.png" />

3. Click **Create Instance**, select the UNS data model that controls the model animation, and then click **Next**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68.png" />

4. Enter the **Instance Name**, set the **Real-world Height** of the model, and click **Create**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/81.png" />

### Motion Mapping
Bind the UNS data to movable parts of the model.
1. On the **Model Mapping** tab, click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/82.png" />, and select the axis of the model position coordinatesto be mapped.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/83.png" />

2. Map the data field with the selected axis.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/84.png" />

3. Do the same for **Rotation Binding** to determine how the model parts rotate according to the data change.

### Static Mapping
Bind the UNS data with the model as a static tag.
:::info
Gaussian models are only available for static mapping, as they have no movable parts.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/118.png" />

:::

1. Click **Data Tagging**, and click **Add Tag**.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/85.png" />

2. Click on the model to select the point the tag represents, and configure the tag attributes.

    <img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/86.png" />

3. Set the motion tags visibility mode, and click **Preview**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/87.png" />


## Viewing Digital Twin Models
1. After mapping UNS data to the model, click **Generate QR Code** at the upper-right corner.
2. Select **Share Model**, and click **Generate**.

<img width={450} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/88.png" />

3. Scan the QR code to view the digital twin model on your mobile device.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/89.png" />

:::info
- When the model is added to a scene, share the scene to view the model with position, lighting and other settings.
- You can also download the code, or copy the code link for later use.
:::

## Quick Operations during Mapping
:::info
Node mappings are saved automatically.
:::
#### On the Model Window

- Click **Replace Model** at the upper-right corner to replace the model while keeping the mapping settings after analysis.
- Drag the slider of **Exploded View** at the bottom of the model window to view the model parts.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-2.png" /> to display the model in its original state.
- Click <img width={25} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/68-3.png" /> to view the model in full screen.

#### On the Mapping Tab

- Click **Edit Nodes** at the upper-right corner to modify selected nodes.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/70.png" /> to disconnect a model node from the instance.
- Click <img src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/6.png" /> to add payload to UNS topics for quick mapping with new data.

<img width={350} src="https://tier0saas1.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/71.png" />
